Hui Tang 唐辉

Ph.D. Student in Ecological Economics · University of Groningen

Hui Tang is a Ph.D. student at the Energy and Sustainability Research Institute Groningen (ESRIG), University of Groningen, the Netherlands. His research focuses on ecological planning, biodiversity conservation and the impacts of global trade on ecosystems. He received his M.S. in Geography from Peking University (2025) and his B.M. in Land Resource Management from China Agricultural University (2022).
