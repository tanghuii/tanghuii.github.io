Hui Tang

Ph.D. Student in Ecological Economics · University of Groningen

I am a Ph.D. student at the Energy and Sustainability Research Institute Groningen (ESRIG), University of Groningen, the Netherlands. My research focuses on biodiversity conservation and the impacts of global trade on ecosystems. I received my M.S. in Geography from Peking University (2025) and my B.M. in Land Resource Management from China Agricultural University (2022). I was awarded the China National Scholarship at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels.
